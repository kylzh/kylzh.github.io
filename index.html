About me

Hi there! I'm Kayla, a data journalist and content writer based in Canada. My bread and butter is turning complex data into clear, compelling stories that actually get read.

My work spans data scraping and collection, data analysis, data visualization, and edtorial writing, whether that's a full research report, a data-driven editorial piece, or a survey findings blog post, I bring the same care to the numbers as I do to the words.

Currently, I work full-time at Visualping where I handle PR and marketing across a mix of everything: content marketing, pitching to journalists, social media marketing, building media relationships, and everything in between.

Outside of that, I take on freelance data storytelling projects for clients who need someone who can do it all.

If you have data that deserves a better story, I'd love to hear about it.
